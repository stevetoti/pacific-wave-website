## Measurement

Review non-brand impressions/clicks by service page and country, GBP website visits tagged with `utm_campaign=gbp`, and qualified enquiries. Compare equivalent complete periods; note that GA4 collection started with the repair on 17 October and initial realtime traffic includes QA. Do not interpret fresh tracking as a sudden business-growth increase.

A contact click is intent, not a completed enquiry. Conversion instrumentation needs a successful submission and duplicate-event/privacy checks before adopting it as a lead KPI. No paid keyword service, ad campaign or recurring automation is created by this rollout.

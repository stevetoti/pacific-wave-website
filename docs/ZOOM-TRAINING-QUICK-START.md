## Stephen / instructor

1. Open Zoom Workplace and sign in to the Pacific Wave Digital paid account.
2. Open the meeting **PWD — Build Your Online Business in 30 Days — October Training** (meeting ID **962 8326 1755**) and click **Start**. Opening it about 15 minutes early gives you time to check sound and screen sharing.
3. Check your microphone, speaker and camera. Admit your learners from the waiting room; they enter muted.

5. To record, use Zoom desktop **Record on this Computer**. The existing cloud recording storage is full. After class, upload the recording through the course’s lesson editor and save it.
6. At the end, choose **End meeting for all**.

[Open the host meeting settings](https://zoom.us/meeting/96283261755)

## Students

1. Sign in at [Training Centre](https://pacificwavedigital.com/training-center).
2. Open **Build Your Online Business in 30 Days**, then **Live class 1**.
3. Click **Join inside dashboard**, review Zoom’s terms, and click **Join**.
4. Wait for the instructor to admit you. Allow browser microphone access when you want to speak; camera is optional and uses more data.
5. Use **Return to course** when finished. If the embedded classroom cannot connect, use **Open in Zoom**.

Approved/paid access is required. The meeting passcode is provided automatically by the dashboard. Avoid sharing the meeting invitation publicly.
